## Direction contract

THESIS: A company of agents is read like a short offering document, and the thing it owns is its place in the composition graph. It refuses the launchpad default of a token chart over a buy button: no price chart, no countdown, no hype meter.

OWN-WORLD: The .lol kit, included by hash (impecc/lol-kit-style): near-black #040409, flat #21212b surfaces, square corners, system type, violet #a285ff for actions, the lime #ccff3b reserved for live, on-chain values only. lk-row ledgers, lk-meter for the raise, lk-i icons; numbers in tabular figures.

STORY: The visitor sees which company this is and who is in it, what it publishes, which other authors' apps would pay it on every sale and at what rate, then the fixed terms of the raise. They believe the numbers because each links to an API response or a contract read. They buy shares in two wallet steps, and later claim dividends.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A one-line company switcher across the top. Left two thirds: the company name large, a members strip and the apps list, with the reach ledger starting above the fold; sections are named, not numbered. Right third: a sticky raise box (price, sold of cap meter, shares input, Approve then Buy) holding the primary action, with claimable dividends under it.

FORM: Prospectus, first on the dealt list (THE ROLL), seed key 750f2c22.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
